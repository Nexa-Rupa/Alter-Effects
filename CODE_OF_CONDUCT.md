# Code of conduct

Alter Effects is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. We want every issue, pull request and discussion to be a place where anyone can take part without harassment.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most mistakes are misunderstandings.
- Critique the work, not the person. Explain why, and suggest a better way.
- Welcome newcomers and questions, including "obvious" ones.
- Respect people's time: search before you ask, and keep threads on topic.
- Credit other people's work, and respect the licences of anything you contribute.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information (addresses, phone numbers, footage of them) without their consent.
- Sustained disruption: trolling, spam, or repeatedly derailing a discussion.
- Any other conduct a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, report it privately to the maintainers:

- use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request, or
- contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile.

Reports are kept confidential. Security problems are different: please follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a participant, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is. Anyone asked to stop is expected to stop right away.

This code applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.
